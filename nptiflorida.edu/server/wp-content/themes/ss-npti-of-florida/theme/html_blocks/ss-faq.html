NPTI Florida: The FAQ

  1. 1. What is HYFLEX learning?

    HyFlex (Hybrid-Flexible) learning is an educational model combining in-person and online instruction, giving students the choice to attend class face-to-face, synchronously online (live), or asynchronously (recorded/on-demand) for each session, ensuring equal access and learning opportunities regardless of chosen mode. It differs from standard hybrid models by offering daily student choice, adapting to needs like health issues, scheduling conflicts, or location, and requiring instructors to facilitate high-quality experiences in all formats.

  2. 2. Does NPTI Accept Veteran Benefits?

    Yes, NPTI Florida accepts veteran benefits. We work with veterans to navigate available education benefits including GI Bill® and other VA programs. Contact our admissions team for details on how to apply your benefits toward your personal training certification program.
