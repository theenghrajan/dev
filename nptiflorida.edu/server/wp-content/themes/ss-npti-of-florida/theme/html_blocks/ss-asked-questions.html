Frequently Asked Questions

Our streamlined enrollment process makes advancing your career simple and effective.

Do I need prior certification?

Yes, a base personal training certification is preferred, though we consider applicants with equivalent experience on a case-by-case basis.

How long is the course?

The strap training specialization course is designed to be completed efficiently alongside your existing schedule. Contact our admissions team for the current duration and upcoming start dates.

What equipment is needed?

You will need access to suspension training straps (such as TRX® or equivalent). NPTI Florida provides guidance on recommended equipment, and straps are available for use during in-person sessions at our campuses.

Can I offer online sessions with this certification?

Yes. Strap training is highly adaptable and works well in online coaching formats. Clients only need their own set of straps and a suitable anchor point, making it a popular choice for virtual personal trainers.

Is continuing education required?

Continuing education requirements depend on your base certification. NPTI Florida offers a range of continuing education courses to help you maintain and expand your credentials over time.
