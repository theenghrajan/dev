What is Strap Training?

Strap training utilizes resistance bands, TRX or suspension straps, and body-weight exercises to build strength, stability, and mobility. It's ideal for clients seeking efficient full-body workouts, home-based fitness, rehabilitation support, or functional fitness training.

Full-Body Workouts

Comprehensive training that targets multiple muscle groups with minimal equipment.

Home-Based Fitness

Perfect for clients who prefer working out at home or have limited space.

Rehabilitation Support

Low-impact exercises suitable for clients recovering from injuries.

Functional Fitness

Training that improves movement patterns for everyday activities.
