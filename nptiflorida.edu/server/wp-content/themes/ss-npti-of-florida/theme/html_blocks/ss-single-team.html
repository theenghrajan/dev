Owner/VA Educational Specialist

Patrick Sherman

Patrick Sherman is a dedicated fitness and wellness enthusiast with a passion for helping individuals achieve their fitness career and health goals. He has been helping staff large and small health clubs and fitness facilities with qualified educated Trainers for over 25 years.

Professional Journey

With over 25 years of experience in the health and fitness industry, Patrick began his career in the US Navy at the age of seventeen, after serving Patrick worked as a defense contractor with the US and foreign military forces. His team was responsible for fleet readiness during Desert Storm and Desert Shield. Secret-Top Secret security clearances were held. Traveled extensively Worldwide and lived in nine countries for a 10-year period.

In 2000, Patrick took the bold step of opening NPTI Florida, a premier accredited training institution focused on providing high-quality personal training and nutrition education. Under his leadership, NPTI Florida has gained recognition for its comprehensive accredited training programs, which empower personal trainers to excel in their Fitness careers.

Patrick's training philosophy centres on evidence-based programming, functional movement, and long-term client success over quick results.

As a veteran himself, Patrick is deeply committed to supporting transitioning service members. NPTI Florida proudly serves as a veteran-owned, operated, and staffed institution.

Outside of work, Patrick is passionate about fitness, travel, and mentoring the next generation of fitness professionals.
