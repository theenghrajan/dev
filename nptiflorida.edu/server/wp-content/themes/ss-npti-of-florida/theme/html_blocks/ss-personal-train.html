Competitive Salaries

Personal trainers have the potential to earn competitive salaries in the field of personal training. According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, as of May 01, 2023, the median annual wage for fitness trainers in the United States is $65,790. The average salary range typically falls between $47,463 and $80,820, depending on factors such as experience, location, and specialization. By pursuing a career in personal training, you have the opportunity to not only make a positive impact on people's lives but also achieve financial stability and success.
