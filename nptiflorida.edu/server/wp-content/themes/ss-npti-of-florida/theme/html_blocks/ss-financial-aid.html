Financial Aid & Admissions for NPTI Students

Admissions

Applicants for the Personal Fitness training program must meet the following requirements for consideration for admission.

Submit an Application For Admission with a $50.00 registration fee. The $50.00 registration fee is non-refundable.

Be at least 18 years of age.

Be a U.S. Citizen or have a green card.

Provide a high school diploma or equivalent.

Personal interview is required.

Be of good character.

Our school does not discriminate against any applicant on the basis of race, gender, age, or disability in accordance with federal and state guidelines.
