About Our Health and Wellness Coaching Program

Health and wellness coaching focuses on helping people improve their physical, mental, and emotional well-being. Our program equips you with the knowledge, skills, and tools to guide clients toward lasting lifestyle changes and healthier habits.

Our 600-hour curriculum stands apart by emphasizing in-depth education in anatomy, physiology, nutrition, and behavioral change — combined with hands-on practical experience that prepares you for real-world success in the fitness industry.

As a military-trusted school, we proudly support veterans and active-duty service members in transitioning to a rewarding career in health and wellness. Our program is designed to meet your needs and help you achieve your goals.
