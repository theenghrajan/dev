NPTI Florida (Orlando/Tampa)
Job Placement Assistance Services

The NPTI Florida's Secure Fitness Job board provides the most up to date job openings for Certified Health and Fitness Professionals. Graduates can Search jobs and find the right personal training career opportunity available Nationwide. Graduates receive a secure login at graduation in order to access Fitness industry career openings.

If you are an owner, manager, assistant manager or oversee your clubs personal training department. NPTI Florida would love to help you with your staffing needs. We have been assisting clubs nationwide for over 25 years. Please submit your job description/overview to pat@nptifl.com and we will post it on our secure graduate career board. Please submit as much information and contact details as possible. This will allow for us to find the correct motivational fit for your facility and clientele. We work with Fitness facilities large and small from around the Globe.

If you are a local Fitness facility and would like to visit NPTI Florida (Orlando/Tampa) and speak to a graduating class. We would love to schedule a time for a brief meet and greet and allow you to describe the features and benefits of your facility and personal training opportunities. You can also interview on site afterwards to all interested candidates.
