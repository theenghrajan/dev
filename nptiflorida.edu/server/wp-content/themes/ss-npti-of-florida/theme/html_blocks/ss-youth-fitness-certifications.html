How to Become a Health Coach

Explore Our Programs: Take a look at our health coach certification program available in Orlando. Choose the semester that suits your needs and aspirations.

Contact Us: Reach out to our admissions team for any questions or clarifications you may have. We're here to help you make an informed decision about your career path.

Enroll: Once you're ready, enroll in our program. We'll assist you in the enrollment process and provide all the necessary information to get started.

Apply for FAFSA: Complete the FREE Application for Federal Student Aid using school code 042505.

Attend Classes: Join our in-person classes and engage in a comprehensive curriculum designed to equip you with the knowledge and skills needed to excel as a health coach.

Network: Take advantage of the networking opportunities available to you. Connect with instructors, fellow students, and industry professionals to expand your network and enhance your career prospects.

Certification: Upon successful completion of our program, you'll earn your Health Coach Certification, setting you on the path to a rewarding and fulfilling career.
