Understanding Your Estimate

The "Net Price" is an estimate of what you may pay for one academic year after subtracting gift aid (money you don't have to pay back). It typically includes:

  • Tuition & Required Fees: The direct cost of your program.

  • Books, Supplies & Equipment: Estimated costs for your coursework.

  • Housing & Meals: An estimate for living expenses, whether on-campus, off-campus, or commuting.

  • Minus Grant or Scholarship Aid: This includes need-based and merit-based aid that does not require repayment.
