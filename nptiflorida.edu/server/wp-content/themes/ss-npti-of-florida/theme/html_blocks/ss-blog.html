Patrick Sherman

-Author/Blog Contributor

Patrick Sherman is an author/Blog Contributor and accomplished fitness sales trainer, strategist, speaker, and entrepreneur with the National Personal Training Institute (NPTI), where he brings together 25 years of industry expertise, practical experience, and a results-driven approach to professional development. With a strong background in personal training education and fitness business strategy, Patrick specializes in teaching how to effectively communicate value, build client trust, and grow sustainable careers in the fitness industry.

As an author, he focuses on translating complex sales and training concepts into clear, actionable insights that empower fitness professionals to succeed both on the gym floor and in sales consultations. Recently written The Personal Trainer's Playbook: Sales Approach for Maximum Impact.
