## Review
Cross-checked against the vendor docs (woocommerce.com/document/cancellation-surveys-offers-woocommerce-subscriptions):
- **Correct:** the docs mention no email or notification on submission. The page columns are Survey Answer, User, Subscription, Survey & Offer, Retain Status, Offered Coupon, Date Created and Action, and the only action is delete. Each reason can have "Ask for details in the text field" turned on, which is where the free text comes from.
- **Missed:** the docs say "Survey responses are also recorded in the subscription Order Notes." Staff can therefore see the note on the subscription itself, not only on this screen.
- **Missed:** the answer doesn't flag the 4 pages already there. Any "please change my account/order" note in them was never routed to anyone, so someone should go through the backlog once. The Retain Status column shows whether each customer actually cancelled or took the offer.
- **Missed, a no-code option:** WooCommerce Subscriptions has a built-in admin "Cancelled Subscription" email (WooCommerce -> Settings -> Emails). Pointing its recipient at contact@ would alert the inbox on every cancellation, and staff can then open the subscription notes to read the survey answer. The email doesn't contain the survey text, and taking a retention offer doesn't send it. Not verified on the live site (current recipient unknown).
- **Tone:** comment #1 is written for developers (wp_mail, slugs, row-action code). Eric/Tennessee need a plain-language version.
- The agent's claim that there's no `wp_mail()` in the plugin can't be re-checked from here (premium plugin, no source). The docs don't contradict it.
