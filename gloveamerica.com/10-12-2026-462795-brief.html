3. How the ad tracking works (what Diana confirmed in #31)

Why one source matters: the link and the forms read the same cookies, so they can never credit one lead to two different ad clicks.
Why "clean ID" matters: Google's own cookie stores GCL.1728….abc123. Uploading that would be rejected. These cookies store just abc123.
Bookings: the calendar can't carry hidden fields, so Diana's GTM tag watches for a finished booking and sends the email + click ID to HubSpot itself.
Our only rule: don't change how the calendar is embedded (standard HubSpot code), or that GTM tag stops working.
