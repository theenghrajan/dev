### Row 1 — H2: How Your Gold and Silver Are Priced
```html
<p>Every offer starts with the spot price, the live market price of pure metal. Your piece is rarely pure, though, so we work out how much actual gold or silver it holds before the spot price comes into it. Our <a href="/how-is-the-price-of-gold-determined-a-guide-for-los-angeles-buyers-sellers/">guide to how the price of gold is determined</a> covers the longer version. Here is what goes into your number:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Weight.</strong> We weigh each item on our scale and show you the reading, in grams or troy ounces.</li>
<li><strong>Purity.</strong> A karat stamp only tells part of the story, so we test the metal itself instead of trusting the mark.</li>
<li><strong>Spot price.</strong> The market price moves through the day, so an offer reflects today and not last week.</li>
<li><strong>Type and condition.</strong> Bullion coins and bars track spot closely. Collectible coins can carry a premium for rarity and grade, and a dented or broken piece of jewelry is valued mostly for its metal.</li>
</ul>
<p>You see those numbers before you decide anything. The appraisal is free, and nothing obliges you to sell.</p>
```
